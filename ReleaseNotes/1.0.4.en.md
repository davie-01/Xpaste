## XPaste 1.0.4

- New: Settings › Permissions shows whether Device Control and Data Access and Full Disk Access are allowed, with links to the matching System Settings.
- History retention adds 3 Months and 6 Months.
- Fixed: pressing Return in the confirmation for deleting several items did nothing. Confirmations now follow one rule: Return performs actions you can undo and cancels ones you can't.
- Fixed: onboarding kept asking to open Full Disk Access settings after access was granted.
