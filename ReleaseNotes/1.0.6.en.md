## XPaste 1.0.6

- Text copied from Safari no longer shows as white blocks in dark mode.
- Images inside copied web content now appear on cards and in previews.
- Formatted text copied from WPS now shows its formatting on cards and in previews.
- The space-bar preview always opens at the top.
- Switching cards with the arrow keys while the preview is open is smoother.
- The card list now slides smoothly when the arrow keys reach the edge of the screen.
